# Non-Fatiguing Audio & Sound Design Reference

> **Executable Pure-Code SFX Engine**: This plugin ships with the sibling **`saas-code-sfx`** skill (`../../saas-code-sfx/SKILL.md`), which implements these exact anti-fatigue & anti-piercing rules in deterministic Python (`scripts/sfx_engine.py`) and enforces them automatically via an 8-gate DSP auditor (`scripts/audit_sfx.py`). Use it whenever synthesizing SaaS UI/motion SFX in code.

## 1. Why Typical AI Video Audio Causes Listener Fatigue

Most motion design audio generated by automated tools suffers from **Acoustic Irritation Syndrome**:
1. **High-Frequency Aggression**: Sharp 3 kHz–5 kHz synthesized beeps and clicks that sting the eardrum when played repeatedly.
2. **Repetition Bias**: The exact same "whoosh" or "pop" sample triggered 10+ times in 15 seconds.
3. **No Frequency Carving**: Sounds stacked directly on top of each other in the 500 Hz–1 kHz mid-range, resulting in muddy, grating noise.
4. **Lack of Organic Foley**: Using 100% synthetic laser sweeps rather than acoustic, real-world physical textures.

## 2. The Non-Fatiguing Sound Palette

High-end editorial films (Apple, Teenage Engineering, OpenAI, Pentagram) use **organic, physical, dampened foley**.

### A. The 5 Core Organic Foley Categories
1. **Archival Paper & Tactile Material**:
   - Heavy 300gsm cotton rag paper sliding across oak veneer.
   - Smooth card flick / deck shuffle (subtle, dry, close-mic).
   - Use on: 3D card cascades, slide transitions, dossier reveals.
2. **Precision Mechanical Optics**:
   - Leica mechanical shutter actuation (single crisp dampened thud, no electronic beep).
   - Precision brass dial turning 1 click.
   - Glass slide meeting microscope stage clip.
   - Use on: Aperture openings, camera zoom locks, focal shifts.
3. **Deep Resonant Acoustics**:
   - Muted marimba or wooden log drum strike (fundamental at 90 Hz – 180 Hz).
   - Sub-bass cavitation pulse (smooth 45 Hz sine envelope with 60ms decay).
   - Use on: Scene pivot points, major headline impacts.
4. **Fluid & Molecular Micro-Textures**:
   - Viscous fluid surface tension release.
   - Microscopic effervescence (low-passed fizz, not harsh white noise).
   - Use on: Biological specimens, cellular growth, crystal dissolution.
5. **Dampened Magnetic Switches**:
   - Subdued magnetic contact tap (like MacBook MagSafe docking).
   - Use on: Vector target bracket locks, HUD coordinates appearing.

## 3. Decibel Levels & Frequency Carving (The Mixing Matrix)

To ensure the audio is pleasant on both studio monitors and phone speakers:

```
0 dBFS  ------------------------------------------------------------- [CLIPPING CEILING]
-3 dBFS ------------------------------------------------------------- [Master Limiter Ceiling]
-6 dBFS ------------------------ [Primary Music / Drone Bed Peak]
-10 dBFS ----------------------- [Sub-Bass Transition Hits]
-14 dBFS ----------------------- [Tactile Paper & Mechanical Foley]
-18 dBFS ----------------------- [HUD Ticks, Anchor Dots, Micro-Transients]
-24 dBFS ----------------------- [Room Tone / Ambient Tape Hum]
```

### Parametric EQ Curves:
- **Low-Cut (High-Pass)**: Filter out everything below 35 Hz on SFX to prevent subwoofer mud.
- **High-Shelf Cut**: Attenuate frequencies above 7 kHz on HUD clicks by `-4dB` to `-8dB` to eliminate harsh ear-fatiguing transients.
- **Mid-Range Notch**: Dip `-2.5dB` at 2.5 kHz to 3.5 kHz (the human ear's most sensitive and easily fatigued frequency band).

---

## 4. Spacing & Pacing Rules

- **Maximum Sound Density**: Never trigger more than **2 sound events within any 0.5-second window** unless it is a single cohesive mechanical sound (like a shutter actuation which naturally contains a mirror lift + curtain drop).
- **The Golden Silence Rule**: At least 30% of visual micro-movements must happen **in silence**. If every button, line, and letter has a sound, the viewer tunes out and gets annoyed. Let the visuals carry the subtlety.
