# Haute Motion Director (`SKILL.md` — Multi-Archetype Auteur Edition)

## 0. THE CORE DIRECTIVE: UNRESTRICTED CREATIVE DIVERSITY (ZERO SLOP)

HyperFrames is an unrestricted code-to-video runtime. **You are strictly forbidden from reusing a single visual template across different projects.**

When directing and coding a video, you must never default to a single formula (e.g. microscope apertures and 3D card fans). Instead, you must interrogate the product or narrative, select the **one archetype** that perfectly fits its soul, and engineer an original visual language from scratch.

### The 6 Deadliest Hallmarks of "AI Motion Slop" (PERMANENTLY BANNED):
1. **The Cyberpunk Slop**: Neon magenta-cyan glows, glowing Tron grids, futuristic holographic rings, and dark purple rounded cards.
2. **The "Greyish Toggle" Slop**: Muddy, low-contrast grey overlay pills (`rgba(100,100,100,0.3)` or `#2A2A2A`), generic Figma toggle-switch widgets, and dull floating rounded boxes.
3. **The "Feature Eyebrow Pill" & Glowing Status Dot Slop**: Dark rounded stadium pills (`border-radius: 999px`, `#18181b` with 1px border) containing uppercase monospace green or white text (`SMART FORMATTING ENGINE`, `● SYSTEM-WIDE DICTATION`) with glowing LED status dots (`box-shadow: 0 0 8px #22c55e`). This is the #1 dead giveaway of cheap 2023 AI website templates and destroys auteur credibility.
4. **The "Red vs. Green Strikethrough Diff Box" Slop**: Stacked comparison cards with artificial thin red (`#ef4444`) and green (`#22c55e`) outlines, uppercase monospace corner headers (`SPOKEN STREAM UNFILTERED` vs `OUTPUT AUTO-FORMATTED`), and red strikethrough tags on filler words (`~~um~~`, `~~uh~~`). This looks like a low-effort PowerPoint slide. High-end motion shows transformations organically in-situ within the actual native UI or through pure typographic evolution.
5. **The Audio Slop**: Continuous high-frequency digital bleeps, identical whooshes triggered on every single word, and royalty-free EDM risers.
6. **The Copywriting Slop**: Clichéd marketing buzzwords (*"Unleash the power"*, *"Supercharge your workflow"*, *"The future of X is here"*).

## 1. THE CONSTITUTION OF TASTE (60+ HARD NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS)

Audit every storyboard, color token, motion curve, and sound design stem against these rules:

### A. Color & Material Bans
1. **NEVER** use neon magenta (`#FF00FF`), electric cyan (`#00FFFF`), or bright neon purple (`#A855F7`).
2. **NEVER** use dark slate/navy (`#0F172A`, `#1E293B`) as a dark canvas. Use deep carbon (`#0A0A0A`), pure obsidian (`#000000`), rich graphite (`#121212`), or warm anodized black (`#141416`).
3. **NEVER** use dull, muddy grey overlays or grey toggle-like rounded boxes. Floating UI surfaces must be **crisp solid white** (`#FFFFFF` with `1px solid #E5E5E0`), **pure obsidian** (`#000000` with `1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.2)`), or **crystal-clear optical glass** (`backdrop-filter: blur(32px); border: 1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.4)`), never murky low-contrast `#333333` / `#444444` floating blobs.
4. **NEVER** apply CSS gradients to typography (`background-clip: text; color: transparent`). All typography must be solid ink: pure white, carbon black (`#111111`), warm bone (`#F4F4F0`), or crisp titanium.
5. **NEVER** float blurred colored Gaussian blobs behind text.
6. **NEVER** use glowing `box-shadow` with high opacity. Shadows on cards must be ultra-diffused: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.85)` on dark, or `0 20px 48px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)` on light.
7. **NEVER** mix warm organic cream tones with cool digital cyan. Keep the palette temperature locked per scene.

### B. Typography & Copywriting Bans
8. **NEVER** write generic AI marketing clichés (*"Unleash"*, *"Supercharge"*, *"Revolutionize"*, *"Next-gen"*, *"Seamless"*, *"Game-changer"*). Write concrete, human, functional copy.
9. **NEVER** use loose letter-tracking on large headlines. Sans-serif headlines (`48px – 80px`) require tight tracking (`-0.035em` to `-0.045em`) and line-height `1.0` to `1.08`.
10. **NEVER** make body text paragraphs wider than `42ch` to `48ch`.
11. **NEVER** animate text with cheesy cartoon rubber bounces or elasticity. Use crisp mechanical cuts, Gary Provost rhythmic staccato pops, or linear/power2 directional wipes.
12. **NEVER** place text over a busy visual without a dedicated contrast backing.

### C. Audio & Sound Design Bans (The Anti-Fatigue & Anti-Piercing Rules)
13. **NEVER** repeat the same sound effect more than **twice** in a 20-second span — use polymorphic micro-variation on every sequential trigger.
14. **NEVER** use raw single-sine digital beeps, tuning-fork pings (`800–4000 Hz` narrowband spike `> 24 dB`), or harsh high-frequency content (`5–9 kHz > 3%`).
15. **NEVER** place an audio whoosh on every word or minor layout change. Audio transients are reserved **strictly for major visual scene shifts or macro camera surges**.
16. **NEVER** use generic stock EDM, upbeat corporate ukulele whistling, or aggressive dubstep risers.
17. **NEVER** have abrupt clipping on audio head/tail or startling full-scale intro chime peaks (`0–2s` intro peak must stay `<= -4.5 dBFS`, felt-mallet attack `>= 18 ms` on chimes / `>= 55 ms` on opening chord blooms). Every audio asset must have smooth raised-cosine micro-fade in/out.
18. **THE 30% SILENCE RULE**: At least 30% of visual transitions must occur in **complete acoustic silence** to preserve dynamic contrast and prevent ear fatigue.

### D. UI Component & Layout Bans (The Anti-SaaS Slop Rules)
19. **NEVER** use floating stadium pill badges (`border-radius: 999px`) or capsule containers as category/feature eyebrows above headlines (e.g. `SMART FORMATTING ENGINE`, `● SYSTEM-WIDE DICTATION`). If an eyebrow or category tag is needed, use pure, unboxed typography (a quiet, small uppercase sans-serif line in `Inter` or an editorial serif with no capsule container).
20. **NEVER** add glowing colored LED indicator dots (`●` with `box-shadow: 0 0 8px #22c55e`) inside pills or next to labels as pseudo-telemetry decoration in consumer or productivity software.
21. **NEVER** construct artificial "Before-and-After" or "Diff" comparison cards with color-coded red (`rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.3)`) and green (`rgba(34, 197, 94, 0.4)`) borders, corner metadata labels (`RAW / UNFILTERED` vs `CLEAN / FORMATTED`), and strikethrough text on filler words (`~~um~~`, `~~uh~~`). This looks like a cheap Canva infographic. Show intelligence and transformations organically inside the real native application window (real cursor backspacing, typing replacement, or inline morphing) or through clean typographic cut-ins.
22. **NEVER** force monospace typography (`Space Mono`, `Courier`, `JetBrains Mono`) into non-developer consumer software headers, feature pills, or marketing callouts. Monospace is strictly reserved for genuine code syntax, terminal CLIs, or authentic financial ticker data.

### E. Motion Capture & Static Analysis Bans (The Anti-Screenshot Trap)
23. **NEVER analyze motion by sparse static snapshots alone**: Sampling only 1 or 2 static screenshots per scene or at arbitrary multi-second intervals creates a catastrophic "static slide" blind spot. It captures resting layouts but completely blinds the director to in-between motion graphics: active UI feed scrolling, live ticker counters, micro-interaction click presses, staggered cascades, and kinetic typographic pacing.
24. **NEVER leave interfaces in dead static states**: In an auteur motion piece, software is alive. When a UI screen is displayed, it must exhibit organic, purposeful kinetic life: active content scrolling, live cursor hover/click actuation, numerical incrementation, or subtle 3D perspective drift. Never let an interface sit as an inert PNG mockup.
25. **NEVER treat kinetic typography as a single static block or fade**: Multi-word phrases must never just fade in all at once as a single flat element. Words and syllables must arrive with intentional musical cadence—staggered kinetic reveals, Gary Provost rhythmic pops, or directional push-ins snapped to the soundtrack's percussive transients.
26. **NEVER ignore interactive state morphs**: When a user or system interaction occurs (e.g. "Follow back", "Buy", "Deposit", "Submit"), never cut to the new state without showing the tactile micro-interaction (scale depression on click, instant button state morph, and haptic feedback recoil).

## 3. THE HIGH-DENSITY CONTINUOUS MOTION FORENSICS PROTOCOL

### The Fallacy of Sparse Snapshots
Static screenshot sampling (e.g. taking 1 frame at $t=1\text{s}$, $t=3\text{s}$) is catastrophic for motion design. It creates a "slide presentation" bias: it shows what a scene looks like when everything has already arrived, but leaves the director completely blind to:
- The acceleration curves, deceleration damping, and physical inertia.
- In-situ micro-scrolling of feeds, lists, and charts.
- Numerical value ticking and animated vector path growth.
- Button click presses, hover depressions, and state toggles.
- Sequential kinetic typography reveals and staggered layout entrances.

### The Dense Sampling Standard (6–10 Frames Per Shot Minimum)
When deconstructing reference videos, replicating existing motion graphics, or verifying code animations:
1. **Dense Frame Grids**: Divide every single shot or beat into at least **6 to 10 evenly spaced frames** (or sample at $4\text{ to }8\text{ fps}$ across transition windows).
2. **Motion Delta Inspection**: Visually contrast adjacent frames to trace the exact transformation delta between $t_{n}$ and $t_{n+1}$.
3. **The 7 Mandatory Motion Signatures to Hunt & Implement**:
   - **Signature 1: Harmonic Rotational Recoil & Pendulum Physics**: Moving objects (bells, dials, cards, swinging indicators) don't just snap to an angle; they exhibit angular momentum and harmonic ringing (e.g. `rotate: -14deg` $\rightarrow$ `+12deg` $\rightarrow$ `-5deg` $\rightarrow$ `0deg` with `elastic.out` or damped sine curves).
   - **Signature 2: Active In-Situ UI Feed Scrolling**: When an interface arrives, it doesn't freeze. The viewport actively scrolls through content (e.g., wallet balance card scrolling up into the activity feed; candlestick trading view scrolling down into order book or holder lists).
   - **Signature 3: Live Real-Time Data Ticking**: Metrics, token values, counters, and progress indicators must tick dynamically in real-time (`$91.65` $\rightarrow$ `$93.20`), and candlestick bars or spline paths must draw dynamically (`strokeDashoffset`) rather than appear statically drawn.
   - **Signature 4: Tactile Micro-Interactions & State Morphs**: Interactive elements must show the tactile actuation: button scale-down depression (`scale: 0.94`), click state swap (e.g., `"Follow back"` $\rightarrow$ `"Following"`), and haptic spring release.
   - **Signature 5: Staggered Momentum Cascades**: Multi-element groups (card stacks, list rows, badge pills) must NEVER pop onto the screen as a collective unit. They must enter from offscreen with staggered delays ($0.08\text{s} - 0.15\text{s}$ offsets) and exponential braking (`power3.out` / `expo.out`).
   - **Signature 6: Continuous 3D Parallax & Spatial Drift**: 3D spatial grids, isometric cards, and background coordinate planes must maintain continuous, subtle camera translation or dolly drift across their entire duration.
   - **Signature 7: Split Kinetic Typographic Staccato**: Multi-word headlines and brand phrases must arrive with rhythmic kinetic cadence. Word 1 appears on beat 1, word 2 reveals beside it on beat 2, a rapid hard cut snaps to the sub-phrase on beat 3, and the brand mark springs in on the downbeat.

### The "In-Between Motion" Audit Checklist
Before approving or rendering any composition, ask these 5 forensic questions:
- [ ] *Did any number, metric, or chart change value during the shot? Is that change animated smoothly or ticking dynamically?*
- [ ] *Did the container or feed scroll its inner contents?*
- [ ] *Did any button, pill, or interactive control change text or visual state between the head and tail of the cut?*
- [ ] *Are entering elements entering with staggered cascade offsets or popping in as a rigid block?*
- [ ] *Is the headline revealed word-by-word/phrase-by-phrase to the rhythm of the track, or is it a flat fade?*

If any answer reveals static complacency, inject the missing GSAP motion curves immediately.

## 3.5 THE FROM-SCRATCH AUTEUR CREATION ENGINE (THE 4-LAYER SYNTHESIS SYSTEM)

### The Catastrophic "Static Slide" Trap in From-Scratch Creation
When directed to create a video from a GitHub repo, web app URL, product description, or brand thesis with **no reference video**, weak agents fall into the "Static Slide" trap:
1. They take 2–3 raw screenshots of the website.
2. They paste them onto a flat, unstyled black canvas (`#000000` or `#111111`).
3. They add a generic title that fades in as a flat block.
4. They cross-fade between screenshots like a 1998 PowerPoint slide deck.

**THIS IS STRICTLY FORBIDDEN.** An auteur film built from scratch is an original, living cinematic universe. When creating without reference footage, you must construct every scene by weaving the **4 Mandatory Architectural Layers**:

### Layer 2: Kinetic Typography Architecture (`saas-kinetic-typography`)
**Rule: Multi-word phrases, headlines, and metrics can NEVER fade in as a single static block or subtitle.**  
Every from-scratch video MUST implement at least **TWO** of the 5 signature kinetic typography styles across its narrative arc:
1. **Style 1: The Cobalt Dynamic Highlight Box & Fisheye Ribbon Echo**:
   - Solid grotesque ink (`Inter`, `-0.045em` tracking, tight line-height `1.02`).
   - Rather than colored text, wrap key words inside an expanding inverted solid white box (`.highlight-shell`: `background: #FFFFFF; color: #3241CF`) that expands its width via `scaleX: 0 → 1` synced to spoken/audio syllable cadence.
   - Ghost word drift (`x: 0 → -120px`, `opacity: 1 → 0.18`) on camera pan.
   - Climactic punch into a 5-row curved cylindrical ribbon echo (`center row: 148px; flanking rows: 112px, 86px with 0.55/0.25 opacity`).
2. **Style 2: In-Situ Nested Thread & Real-Time String Typing**:
   - Authentic customer/community feedback cards with continuous SVG thread stems (`<line>` connecting to `<circle>` collapse badge and `<path d="M... Q...">` bezier branch).
   - Character-by-character string typing animated via JS proxy counter (`onUpdate: () => el.textContent = fullText.slice(0, Math.round(count))`), NEVER artificial monospace terminal fonts for human reviews.
   - Vertical thread scroll with optical depth-of-field blur (`filter: blur(2.4px) → blur(0px) → blur(2.2px)`).
3. **Style 3: Staccato Voice-Locked Kinetic Headline Reveals**:
   - Gary Provost rhythmic word-by-word delivery: Word 1 appears on transient 1, Word 2 punches in beside it on transient 2, instant velocity-cut to the core thesis.
4. **Style 4: Decelerating Numerical Tickers & Metric Odometers**:
   - Large quantitative metrics (`4x`, `98.4%`, `<14ms`) stepping discretely with Swiss easing (`power3.out`) and resolving lock-in chime confirmation.
5. **Style 5: Curved Category Wheel & AST Monospace Token Streaming**:
   - Reserved exclusively for developer tools, APIs, and syntax: tokenized AST keyword streaming and live blinking full-block cursors (`█`).

---

### Layer 3: Tactile Micro-UI Forensics & 3D Stage Choreography
**Rule: Never paste a static screenshot. Reconstruct UI surfaces with kinetic life.**
1. **Authentic Window Frames**: 1px crisp borders, subtle macOS traffic light dots (`10px`), or frameless studio floating cards.
2. **Live SVG Vector Mouse Pointer**: Real `<path d="M0 0 L0 16 L4 12 L8 20 L11 18 L7 10 L13 10 Z"/>` gliding with cubic-bezier easing (`power2.inOut`), depressing targets with tactile micro-scale (`scale: 0.94` at click moment), and rebounding with spring haptics.
3. **In-Situ Transformation**: Transformations happen *inside the native UI window*—live buffer replacement, caret backspacing, or inline state morphing (`"Follow"` ➔ `"Following"`), NEVER artificial red/green diff comparison boxes.
4. **Continuous 3D Perspective Drift**: Stage canvases maintain continuous spatial perspective (`perspective: 1200px`, `transform: rotateX(12deg) rotateY(-18deg) rotateZ(4deg)` with slow camera dolly).
5. **Staggered Cascade Entrances**: Bento panels, list items, and metric cards cascade with `0.08s–0.15s` offsets and exponential braking (`expo.out`).

---

### Layer 4: Procedural Non-Fatiguing SFX & Anti-Fatigue Audio (`saas-code-sfx`)
**Rule: Never use generic EDM, bleeps, or repetitive whooshes.**
1. Synthesize a 100% bespoke, scale-locked SFX track using `sfx_engine.py` in Python.
2. Align transients to exact visual events (aperture snaps on hero reveal, soft felt-mallet chord blooms on thesis holds, two-stage tactile clicks on button depressions, decelerating counter ratchets on odometer ticks).
3. Require **8/8 PASS** from `audit_sfx.py` (peak $\le -0.8\text{ dBFS}$, crest $\ge 11\text{ dB}$, zero harshness, anti-piercing, and 30% complete acoustic silence).

## 4. THE 4-STAGE EXECUTION RUNBOOK (DUAL-PATHWAY)

Whenever tasked with creating or replicating a video, identify your operational pathway immediately:
- **Pathway A (Replication)**: Reference video or cut provided ➔ Execute Dense Frame Forensics (6–10 frames/shot).
- **Pathway B (From-Scratch Auteur Creation)**: GitHub repo, website URL, product brief, or brand concept provided ➔ Execute The 4-Layer Synthesis System.

---

### Stage 1: Archetype Lock & Storyboard Architecture

#### If Pathway A (Replicating Reference Footage):
1. Execute **Dense Frame Sampling (6–10 frames per cut)** across all shots to identify all 7 Motion Signatures before writing any code.
2. Select the matching Archetype and map exact transformation deltas.

#### If Pathway B (Creating From Scratch):
1. **Interrogate the Product**: Determine the core friction point, the technological breakthrough, the primary interaction loop, and the ultimate value metric.
2. **Lock Archetype**: Explicitly declare **"Selected Archetype: [Name]"** from the 6 World-Class Archetypes.
3. **Author the 4-Act SaaS Storyboard Arc** (Every beat MUST document all 4 layers):
   - **Act 1: The Monumental Problem Hook (`0.0s – 3.5s`)**:
     * *Layer 1*: Procedural Atmospheric Backdrop (e.g. Electric Cobalt Canvas with dashed orbital curves).
     * *Layer 2*: Kinetic Typography Style 1 (Inverted expanding highlight box shell).
     * *Layer 4 SFX*: Felt-mallet `chord_bloom` + `aperture_snap`.
   - **Act 2: The Core Technological Breakthrough (`3.5s – 7.5s`)**:
     * *Layer 1*: Twilight Horizon with 3D Spherical Orbital Meridians or Arctic-Blue Horizon.
     * *Layer 3*: Native UI Window in 3D perspective, live SVG cursor glide + tactile click depression (`scale: 0.94`), real-time input injection.
     * *Layer 4 SFX*: `tactile_click` + `silk_whoosh` trajectory sweep.
   - **Act 3: Interactive Verification & Feature Deep-Dive (`7.5s – 12.0s`)**:
     * *Layer 1*: Deep Space Navy-to-Cyan Matrix Floor Glow.
     * *Layer 2*: Kinetic Typography Style 4 (Decelerating numerical odometer) or Style 2 (In-situ thread typing).
     * *Layer 3*: Morphing bento panels with staggered cascade entrances (`0.10s` offset).
     * *Layer 4 SFX*: `counter_ratchet` + `hover_tick` scrub.
   - **Act 4: Exponential Scale & Brand Resolve (`12.0s – 16.0s+`)**:
     * *Layer 1*: Architectural Micro-Dot Canvas with registration crosshairs.
     * *Layer 2*: Kinetic Typography Style 1 (Fisheye ribbon echo punch) or Style 3 (Gary Provost staccato).
     * *Layer 3*: App icon, official vector wordmark, and primary CTA button with tactile hover/glint.
     * *Layer 4 SFX*: Resolving `chord_bloom` + `bubble_cascade` + final `glass_chime`.

---

### Stage 2: Procedural Asset, Audio & Backdrop Engineering
1. **Audio Synthesis**: Run `sfx_engine.py` via Python (`saas-code-sfx`) using the cue-sheet JSON aligned to the storyboard timestamps. Run `audit_sfx.py` and require **8/8 PASS** before proceeding.
2. **Procedural Backdrop & Shader Code**: Generate or copy the procedural SVG/CSS backdrops from `saas-procedural-backdrops` or initialize the canvas shader engines from `saas-procedural-gradients`.
3. **Vector Specimen & UI Assets**: Extract clean vector icons, product mockups, and high-res UI elements with zeroed padding.

---

### Stage 3: HyperFrames 4-Layer Multi-Composition Coding
1. Initialize root `index.html` with explicit resolution (`1920x1080` or `1280x720`), 30 fps, and discrete scene host containers (`data-composition-src="compositions/sceneN.html"`). Wire the master SFX `<audio id="master-sfx" src="assets/audio/master_sfx.wav">`.
2. Code each scene independently, strictly stacking the 4 layers:
   - Layer 1: Ambient CSS/SVG backdrop mounted to `#root`.
   - Layer 2: Kinetic typography containers with GSAP timelines (`.highlight-shell`, character counters, staccato spans).
   - Layer 3: Interactive UI windows, 3D perspective containers (`perspective: 1200px`), and animated SVG cursor paths.
3. Assign human-readable IDs to all elements. Mark non-reading metadata badges with `data-layout-ignore="true"`.
4. Register each sub-timeline to `window.__timelines["sceneN"]`.

---

### Stage 4: Verification, Auteur Audit & Production Render
1. Run `npx hyperframes lint` -> Require **0 errors**.
2. Run `npx hyperframes check` -> Verify **0 runtime errors**, **0 layout collisions**, and **WCAG AA contrast compliance**.
3. **The From-Scratch Auteur Audit Checklist**:
   - [ ] *Did any scene use a flat background without procedural backdrops, wireframe meridians, or shaders?* ➔ **REJECT & Inject `saas-procedural-backdrops`.**
   - [ ] *Did any headline fade in as a static block without kinetic syllable or word staging?* ➔ **REJECT & Inject `saas-kinetic-typography`.**
   - [ ] *Did the video employ at least two kinetic typography styles from `saas-kinetic-typography`?* ➔ **MANDATORY.**
   - [ ] *Did interactive clicks have tactile scale depression (`scale: 0.94`) and state morphs?* ➔ **MANDATORY.**
   - [ ] *Did the audio pass all 8 gates of `audit_sfx.py`?* ➔ **MANDATORY.**
4. Run dense in-between snapshots across active transition moments (`--at`, `--range`) to verify in-between motion.
5. Run `npx hyperframes render --quality looks -o output.mp4`.
6. Inspect the rendered output with `ffprobe` to ensure exact frame duration, audio synchronization, and zero dropped frames.
