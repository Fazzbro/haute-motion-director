- **HUD Layer**: Top-left monospace tag: `INDEX // 0084.B`. Tiny crosshair at viewport center.
- **Climax ($t = 2.500\text{s}$)**: Single understated word in pure white Inter (`42px`): `[keyword]!` (e.g. `eureka!`, `crystallized.`, `coherent.`).
- **Audio Stem**:
  - `0.0s – 4.5s`: Deep ambient sub-bass drone (`45 Hz`, `-8dB`).
  - `2.5s`: Dampened mechanical shutter click (`-14dB`).

### Beat 2: The Staccato Typography (`4.500s – 5.300s`)

- **Visual Subject**: High-contrast typographic cut.

  - `4.750s – 5.300s`: Word 2: `[The next]` (Inter `68px`, white).
- **Audio Stem**: Dry wooden acoustic transient or tape stop (`-12dB`).

### Beat 3: The Specimen Collage & Target Lock (`5.300s – 8.600s`)
- **Canvas**: `#000000`
- **Visual Subject**: 4–5 floating physical specimens arranged in an intellectual salon layout:
  - Specimen A (Top Left): Raw mineral / geological sample.
  - Specimen B (Top Right): Planetary / celestial body.
  - Specimen C (Bottom Left): Deep marine organism / flora.
  - Specimen D (Center): Live subject with continuous scale tween (`scale: 0.95 -> 1.08`).
- **HUD Layer**:
  - 4-corner SVG vector brackets framing Specimen D (`380x380px`).
  - Rectangular target box framing the word `[discovery / solution]` at $t = 2.8\text{s}$ into scene.
- **Typography**: Anchored text lines: `[The next]`, `[scientific / autonomous]`, `[breakthrough]`.
- **Audio Stem**: Low acoustic marimba hit on entrance (`-10dB`) + muted magnetic lock tick (`-18dB`).
